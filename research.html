Research

Motivation & Adult Development

Motivational processes change systematically across adulthood and play a central role in shaping developmental progress and well-being.

Key Themes

  • Adulthood as a multidimensional and multistage developmental period
  • Developmental progress and well-being
  • Goal-opportunity congruence, diversity, and management of consequences

Work, Career & Retirement

Work trajectories across adulthood reflect how individuals regulate motivation in response to changing opportunities and constraints.

Key Themes

  • Career entry and goal pursuit supports and uncertainty
  • Opportunity beliefs and motivational self-regulation
  • The retirement transition and goal reorganization

Social Mobility & Inequality

What people believe about who succeeds and why shapes the goals they pursue and the roads they take.

Key Themes

  • SES causal beliefs and their behavioral consequences
  • Economic context and opportunity beliefs
  • Civic engagement and political socialization

Social Relationships; Health & Well-being

Health and social relationships direct and reflect the roads individuals take through adulthood.

Key Themes

  • Social relationships, health, and well-being
  • Motivational engagement with health threats
  • Caregiver communication and development
  • Food insecurity and its consequences for student well-being and academics
  • Psychedelic experience and well-being

These roads are still being mapped.
